Learn the U.S. Census Bureau's [four regions and nine divisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Census_Bureau#Regional_divisions) with maps and membership drills.

**13 notes · 70 cards.** Practise highlighted-map recognition, locating a named region or division on a blank map, neighbours, region-to-division and division-to-region relationships, division-to-state membership, and state borders.
